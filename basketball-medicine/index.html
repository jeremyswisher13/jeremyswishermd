Return to play

I can shoot around. When can I play five-on-five?

A solo workout lets you choose when to jump, stop or turn. A defender makes those decisions for you. After an injury, that gap matters. These are three things I consider with a player and their care team.

What happens on the cut?

A straight-line jog tells us less about a recovering ankle than planting for a crossover or sliding to stay in front of a defender. We look at strength, balance and how much you trust the leg.

Can you repeat the effort?

One comfortable jump is a start. Rebounding, landing in traffic and sprinting back on defense ask more. The assessment depends on your injury, your position and your usual minutes.

How does practice carry over?

Modified practice gives us a way to watch symptoms as the pace builds. Your response later that day and the next morning helps guide the conversation about contact and game minutes.
